# Review packet

Use these seven sections, in order. Keep each short; link every figure.

1. **Summary**: project, team, program, status, links to the application and project on app.filpgf.io. For a milestone, add its amount, due and completion dates, and days waiting.
2. **Funding**: committed, disbursed, remaining. For a milestone, its invoice and payment status.
3. **Promised vs done**: each milestone or criterion with a rating — Found, Partial, Not found, Not yet due, or Can't verify — and the evidence link.
4. **Measured**: two or three metrics with baseline, current value and target. Where a team reports a figure that can be checked, a table of reported vs measured.
5. **Context**: recent activity, incidents, reviewer comments on Karma, and earlier reviews (PRIVATE ones linked, not quoted).
6. **Assessment**: per the review type.
7. **Sources**: every source used, with the date read.
